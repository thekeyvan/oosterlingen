How to use the 3D model

  • Drag to rotate · Space + drag (or right-drag) to move · scroll or pinch to zoom · ◎ re-centres the room
  • The camera buttons jump to set views: each room, the plan from above, the balcony, and views inside the kitchen and bathroom
  • From above, the walls are cut at 1.2 m so you can see into every room (switch off under “Show”). Zoom into a room to see the full walls.
  • Blue labels are measured from the drawing, orange labels are assumptions or proposals, red labels are open points
