Shower niche door width. Lokla Linea 140 adjusts 1370–1410 mm. Niche on the drawing is 1400 (scales ≈1385), minus tile + adhesive on both end walls (≈15 + 16 mm) → ≈1369 or less. Measure the finished niche at 3 heights before ordering.
Vanity top. BRAUER Joy is sold without top. Tiled top option: needs a waterproof backer board, edge finish and a Ø40 mm drain hole; with the 60×60 floor tile there is a seam (placed under the basin).
Wall stack at the basin. Basin rim 870 → wall tap ≈975 → 80 cm oval mirror (1020–1820) → wall light point at 1800 sits behind the mirror. Move the light point to ≈1950–2000, or hang the mirror horizontally.
Socket 1200 (≈300–380 from door wall) touches the mirror edge (345). Shift socket ≈10 cm toward the door wall.
Bidet hand shower — drinking-water rules. The Hansgrohe set's own product page says it is not suitable in countries where EN 1717 applies; the Netherlands applies EN 1717 (NEN 1006 + Waterwerkbladen, protection up to fluid class 5), and a hand sprayer that can reach into a WC bowl is treated as class 5. Ask your installer for a compliant set-up before buying. Also needed (not in the set): stop valve, wall outlet, supply pipe in the pre-wall — plan before the pre-wall is closed.
Shelf LEDs need power before tiling. No electrical point above the WC on the drawing. Plan a 24 V LED strip (IP44 or higher — this spot is within ~60 cm of an open walk-in), the driver outside the wet zone (e.g. in the pre-wall behind a service hatch or outside the bathroom), and a switch (could share switch d). Shelf brackets need a solid back wall — check its construction.
In-wall shower thermostat and wall tap need recessed boxes: check that the shower back wall and right wall can be chased (or plan a thin pre-wall).
Metal tones from 4 brands: Hansgrohe "brushed bronze", Aqua Vista / Fortifura / INK "brushed copper", Geberit "brushed red gold" — compare samples side by side.
Shower depth: label 1400, drawing scales ≈1385 mm. Floor area from labels 3.53 m² vs 3.57 m² stated. Verify on site.

Open points

Floor heating + underlay. Klik Patterns PVC has no underlay built in; Flexxfloors' laying manual asks for a PVC-suitable underlay with high compressive strength (> 400 kPa) and a subfloor that stays below 28 °C. Their own PVC underlay (15 dB, 0.007 m²K/W) is in the cost table. Do not lay a second underlay under Silk (it has one built in). Check the VvE rules for a minimum contact-sound improvement before buying — apartment buildings often set one.
Ceiling height for the bathroom. The kitchen drawings give 2640 floor-to-ceiling; the bathroom model still assumes 2600 (bathrooms often get a lowered ceiling — confirm with Vink Bouw).
Tiles vs PVC height. A tiled kitchen (tile + adhesive ≈ 10–12 mm) and click PVC on underlay (5 + 1–2 mm) end at different heights: choose a transition profile that bridges it, or adjust the screed. The model puts the profile in line with the kitchen side of the opening, so the whole opening reads as living-room floor.
Hall ceiling. Many Dutch new-builds lower the hall ceiling for ventilation ducts; the model uses 2640 everywhere. Confirm with Vink Bouw — it changes the wallpaper and paint quantities a little.
Bedrooms, hall and balcony are drawn from floor plan KT-7.135 (scaled, ±30 mm). Door swings, window divisions and the balcony screens follow the plan; window frame details and door heights (2315) are assumed.

Final measurement. The kitchen drawing uses 4003 × 2130; the floor plan says 4010 × 2120. The shop re-measures before delivery week 8/2027 — check fillers then.
Wall not square. The order notes gaps at the shelves because the walls are not at 90° — shelves 20 and 21 are 1238 and 1222 long.

Power for the LED strips. The leidingschema has no point for under-cabinet or shelf lighting. Plan a 230 V point for the LED driver (e.g. hidden in a wall cabinet), a switch or sensor, and run the 24 V cables to the shelves before the backsplash / paint is done — shelves have hidden fixings, so the cable must come out of the wall right at the profile.
Washing machine vs apothecary pull-out. The machine measures 700 mm from the hose connections to the front of the door; the 150 pull-out (pos. 9) slides past its door at ≈686 mm from the wall. Even with the connections against the wall the door is ≈14 mm in the way, so the pull-out only opens ≈15 cm. Raise it with Keuken Concurrent before the final check-measure (options: swap pos. 9 for a filler/shelf, or re-plan the corner).
